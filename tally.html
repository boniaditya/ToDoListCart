Amount spent
i
Adds the saved total of every checked-out order. It includes past purchases, even when their products are now archived.
FormulaAmount spent = Σ saved order totalsTime shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.
Value of checked-out orders
Still to spend
i
Adds the current price of every open item in every cart. Checked-out items are excluded. A product in multiple carts counts once per cart; archived products still in a cart also count.
FormulaStill to spend = Σ current product price for each open cart entryTime shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.
Open items across all carts
Available store value
i
Adds one current price for each active product with at least one unit in stock. The number of units does not multiply its price. Archived and out-of-stock products are excluded.
FormulaAvailable store value = Σ price of active products with units in stock > 0Time shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.
Active products still in stock
Catalog value
i
Adds one current price for every active product, including out-of-stock products. Archived products are excluded. Cart and wishlist membership do not change this amount.
FormulaCatalog value = Σ price of all active productsTime shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.
One price per active product
Archived value
i
Adds one current price for each archived product, whether or not it is in stock. Archived products are outside the active catalog.
FormulaArchived value = Σ price of all archived productsTime shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.
Value of archived products
Wishlist value
i
Adds one current price for each product saved to the wishlist, including archived products. Wishlist items can also appear in carts or the catalog, so these values overlap.
FormulaWishlist value = Σ price of each saved productSaved products = number of unique valid wishlist products. Time shown (if enabled) = amount × configured seconds ÷ configured price.

Store value mix

i
This mix divides the tracked total into checked-out orders, open cart entries, and pending stock. It is a progress view, so its total is different from Catalog value. The percentages use the tracked total as their base.
FormulaSlice % = slice value ÷ Total tracked value × 100When Total tracked value is zero, each percentage is 0%.

Track completed value, items already sitting in carts, and the remaining value still available in the store.

Total tracked value
i
Amount spent + Still to spend + Pending stock. Pending stock is Available store value minus Still to spend, with a minimum of zero. This total can differ from Catalog value because past orders and cart entries are included.
FormulaTotal tracked = Amount spent + Still to spend + max(0, Available store value − Still to spend)
0% complete
Completed
i
The same amount as Amount spent: the saved totals of all checked-out orders. Its percentage is this amount divided by Total tracked value.
FormulaCompleted = Amount spent; Completed % = Completed ÷ Total tracked × 100
0% of total
In carts
i
The same amount as Still to spend: current prices of open entries across all carts. Its percentage is this amount divided by Total tracked value.
FormulaIn carts = Still to spend; In carts % = In carts ÷ Total tracked × 100
0% of total
Pending stock
i
Available store value minus Still to spend, never below zero. Its percentage is the share of Total tracked value left after Completed and In carts.
FormulaPending stock = max(0, Available store value − Still to spend)Pending % = Pending stock ÷ Total tracked × 100
0% of total

Category value

i
For each category, adds prices recorded in past orders, current prices of open cart entries, and remaining in-stock active product value. Remaining stock is in-stock value minus open cart value for that category, never below zero.
FormulaCategory total = order prices + open cart prices + max(0, in-stock active prices − open cart prices)
