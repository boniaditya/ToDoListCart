🛠 HOW IT WORKS

First, create a task product. A product can include a title, description, category, priority, location, images, reference URLs, comments, related products, vendor information, price or value, quantity-style fields, stock-style availability, and status details.

Then, add products to a cart. A cart represents a focused work plan. You can use one cart for today, another for a project, another for errands, another for study, or any structure that fits your workflow.

As you finish work, mark cart items complete and check out the cart. Completed items move into order history, giving you a record of what you finished.

If one item is not moving forward at the expected pace, move it from the cart to the wishlist and keep the active cart focused.
